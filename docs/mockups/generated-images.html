Pictures appear where they were made.

A picture Codex generates shows in its reply, with the prompt it was made from as the caption. Click it to open it full size in the same viewer as an attached image. Switch between the states below.

In the reply

  • The picture sits in the reply's flow, after the steps and before the words that follow it, in the order Codex made them. It is not folded into "Worked · N steps".
  • It is shown up to 520 px wide at its own proportions. The caption is the revised prompt, cut to two lines with More to read the rest.
  • Clicking it opens the existing image viewer (.image-preview). Right-clicking opens the same menu as an attached image: Preview, Save as…, Reveal stored file.
  • While it is being made: a 16:10 field of dots with a slow wave out from the centre, and "Generating image" with the real elapsed time. Codex reports no progress, so there is no bar. After 40 s (three in four of your 113 past images were done by then) it adds "taking longer than usual". With reduced motion on, the dots hold still.
  • When it arrives, the picture comes into focus where the dots were.
  • A failure is one sentence in the error colour, saying what happened and when to try again.
  • Codex looking at a picture is a step like any other: Viewed neon-horizon.png.
